## Text quoted from the Korean notice

The documents quote clause sentences and table values of the notice of the Ministry of Land,
Infrastructure and Transport of the Republic of Korea, 「철도의 건설기준에 관한 규정」
(Rules on Railway Construction Standards). To the authors' understanding, notices of the State are not
protected by copyright under Article 7 of the Copyright Act of the Republic of Korea, so the CC BY 4.0
licence above does not apply to those quoted sentences and values; they can be used freely.
This is the authors' reading, not legal advice.

The quoted text is not an authoritative copy of the notice. Tables are written out as plain text and some
provisos are left out (marked in the documents). Check the official notice before any engineering use.
